The Championship

Six Rounds. Three Days. One Cup.

Grigio Tradition

Golf. Competition. Brotherhood.

For thirteen years, the Grigio Cup has been about more than golf—competition, tradition, questionable decisions, great stories, and the guys who keep coming back year after year.

In 2026, 16 players head to Horseshoe Bay, Texas, for three days of competition across Apple Rock, Ram Rock, and Slick Rock in the Texas Hill Country.

Year 13. Questionable golf. Incredible companionship.
